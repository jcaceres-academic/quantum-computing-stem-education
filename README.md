## 🔁 Research principles

This project follows principles of **reproducibility, transparency, and methodological coherence**.  
All analyses are script-based and designed to ensure traceability of analytical decisions in the study of **quantum computing, data science, and STEM education**.

The repository provides the **conceptual and methodological foundation** of a broader doctoral research programme, informing subsequent applied studies and extensible analytical pipelines, including demonstrative quantum computing extensions under NISQ constraints.
